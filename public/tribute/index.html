01 — THE BITE

AN ORDINARY KID.
AN EXTRAORDINARY FATE.

Bitten by a radioactive spider, Peter Parker gained speed, strength, and a sense that warns him of danger before it strikes. Great power had just found him — and great responsibility was right behind it.

THE LAB

ONE BITE.
EVERYTHING CHANGED.

A school trip, a stray spider, a flash of pain he could barely explain. By morning his vision was sharper, his reflexes faster than thought — his whole body rewritten in a single night.

THE CHOICE

HE DIDN'T ASK
FOR THIS.

Nobody does. But power that shows up uninvited still has to go somewhere — out into the city, or inward until it breaks you. Peter chose the city.

RED. BLUE.
UNMISTAKABLE.

Every thread is a statement. The mask hides his face so anyone — you, your neighbor, your kid down the street — could be the one behind it. That's the whole point.

THE DESIGN

BUILT FROM
FAILURE.

Late nights, torn fabric, web-shooters that jammed more than they fired. The suit wasn't handed to him — he sewed it, wire by wire, until it finally fit his purpose, not just his body.

THE SYMBOL

NOT ARMOR.
A PROMISE.

The web-pattern isn't decoration. It's camouflage, it's identity, and it's a silent promise that whoever's underneath answers to something bigger than their own name.

03 — SWING MECHANICS

Web-slinging isn't flying. It's physics, instinct, and faith in the next line he shoots. No GPS, no shortcuts — just momentum, geometry, and absolute trust that the next rooftop is exactly where it's supposed to be.
